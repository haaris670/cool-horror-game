THE NOOB'S ASYLUM

Support & How to Play

Choose an act, explore the maze, find the key, and reach the exit before the Corrupted Noob catches you.

Before you start: Open the game in a modern web browser with JavaScript enabled. The game runs in the browser, so Windows and Linux players can use a keyboard and mouse, while iPhone and iPad players can use the on-screen touch controls.

🪟 Windows

Use a keyboard and mouse to navigate.

  • W move forward; S move backward.
  • A and D move sideways.
  • Move the mouse to look around. Click the game view to capture the mouse pointer.
  • Hold Shift while moving to sprint. Watch your stamina bar.
  • Press Space or click the pause button to pause or resume.
  • If the pointer is captured, press Esc to release it.

🐧 Linux

Controls are the same as on Windows.

  • W/A/S/D move around the maze.
  • Move the mouse to look; click the game view if needed to capture the pointer.
  • Hold Shift while moving to sprint.
  • Press Space or use the pause button to pause or resume.
  • Use a recent browser with JavaScript and pointer lock enabled for mouse-look.

📱 iOS (iPhone & iPad)

Play in a touch-capable browser. The on-screen controls appear when the game starts.

  • Drag the joystick at the bottom-left to move forward, backward, or sideways.
  • Drag across the game view to look around.
  • Touch and hold SPRINT at the bottom-right while moving to sprint.
  • Tap the pause button in the top-right to pause or resume.
  • For a better experience: Open this site in your browser, tap Share, then scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  • For a better view, rotate your device to landscape orientation.

🎯 Your objective

Select Act 1, Act 2, or Act 3 from the main menu. Find the yellow key shown on the mini-map, then make your way to the exit. Avoid the monster, keep an eye on your stamina, and use the mini-map to plan your route. If you get caught, choose RESPAWN to try again.
